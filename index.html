The field guide / interactive slides

Learn to think like an investigator.

Move through short visual lessons. Each slide introduces a practical principle you can apply in a real investigation.

A working method

Good investigations are built on disciplined questions.

Start with what you can establish. Build a timeline. Correlate independent sources. Keep facts, hypotheses and conclusions separate.

The objective is not to find a suspicious artefact. It is to explain what happened, how you know, and what should happen next.

FIELD NOTE 01 / 04

Observe before you conclude.

An alert is a starting point, not a verdict. Capture the triggering event, identify the host and user, establish the time window, and preserve the original telemetry.
